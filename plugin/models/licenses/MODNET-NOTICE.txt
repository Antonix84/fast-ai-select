MODNet — Trimap-Free Portrait Matting
=====================================

Project:  https://github.com/ZHKKKe/MODNet
Author:   Zhanghan Ke and contributors
License:  Apache License 2.0 (see MODNET-LICENSE.txt in this folder)

Model file bundled with this plugin:
  modnet_photographic_portrait_matting.onnx

The ONNX conversion was obtained from:
  https://huggingface.co/TheEeeeLin/HivisionIDPhotos_matting

The model is used unmodified for on-device portrait matting. It runs locally
through onnxruntime-web; no image or model data leaves the computer.
